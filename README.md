# Why this project ?

India is witnessing a dramatic structural paradox: a massive boom in stock market participation driven by cheap mobile data and fintech apps, alongside severely lagging foundational financial literacy.
India has a severe financial advisory deficit. There are only about 950 SEBI-Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) to serve over 100 million unique retail investors. Human advisory cannot scale to meet this demand. With AI, you can leverage agentic frameworks, generative AI, and predictive modeling to democratize personal finance.
